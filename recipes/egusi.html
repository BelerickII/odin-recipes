Description

The image shows a well prepared tradition Nigerian soup called Egusi

Ingredients

  • Palm Oil
  • Grounded Melon Bean
  • Onions
  • Locust Bean
  • Pepper
  • Ugwu Leaves

Steps

  1. Soak the blended melon seed in water to get a paste mixture
  2. Then fry in heated palm oil alongside chopped onions and pepper mix
  3. Add meat stock, smoked fish and proteins
  4. Simmer for 10-20 minutes
